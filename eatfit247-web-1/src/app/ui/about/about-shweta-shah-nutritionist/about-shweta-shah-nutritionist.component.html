About Shweta Shah — Nutritionist

Shweta Shah is a celebrity nutritionist, renowned for her innovative approach that merges the wisdom of Ayurveda with the precision of modern nutritional science. With a career spanning over 2 decades, she specialises in curating personalised nutrition plans, based on the Ayurvedic Doshas of Kapha, Pitta & Vata.

A postgraduate in Dietetics and Applied Nutrition from Nirmala Niketan, Mumbai, Shweta's impact on the wellness industry is evident in her work — including EatFit24/7, her digital platform venture sharing healthy recipes and tips to inspire a more nutritious lifestyle.

Her initiatives collectively embody her vision of making wellness accessible to all and effortless.
