About Birangi Shah — Chief Nutritionist

Birangi Shah is a passionate and experienced nutritionist at EatFit247 who completed her BSc in Dietetics and Nutrition from SNDT College, Santacruz, in 2003. She has a proven track record of helping clients achieve their nutrition goals and manage their health.

Working with Shweta for the last 16 years, she has practical experience in assessing the needs of individual clients and developing personal meal plans to best promote health — bringing forth a deep understanding of how nutrition and food affect health, weight, and lifestyle.

With her expertise, Birangi educates clients about the benefits of proper nutrition and lifestyle changes, and develops meal plans with thorough information regarding nutrients and recipes. She works closely with clients to monitor progress, motivate them, and address any setbacks or concerns.
