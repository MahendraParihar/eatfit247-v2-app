## Context

The tax engine built in 4.6 and 4.11 reads **rates** from Tax Master, but the **treatment** is code. Treatment covers domestic vs export, CGST+SGST vs IGST, LUT, zero-rated exports and the refusals, all written as `if (supplier === 'IN')` / `if (VAT)` branches in `tax-engine.service.ts`. A review on 2026-10-25 found six limits:

1. HCUAE's export rate is hardcoded 0% zero-rated.
2. `mst_program_plan.is_electronic_service` is stored but no code reads it.
3. Rules are keyed by the supplier's country only.
4. Legal refusals are hardcoded, and the inter-state goods override is an `mst_configs` flag.
5. Some data isn't captured: UAE zero-rating evidence (G7), goods-export documents (G9), and a validated customer GSTIN / TRN (G15).
6. A product has no default rule.

The owner and the CA or adviser decide tax law; the system must let them apply each decision without a deployment. Owner rules (2026-10-25):
- **No tax behaviour or feature switch lives in `mst_configs` or env**, except `DIET_SAC_CODE`, which stays (owner). Each switch lives in the master table that owns it.
- **A franchise states the currency it works in.**
- **Every franchise has its own invoice counters. If the counter a sale needs doesn't exist, the payment entry fails**; no counter is created on the fly.
- **Every answer is validated against the law** (see § Legal Validation).

Three franchises run today: EFMUM (India, services, GSTIN), MEMUM / Mahi (India, goods, not registered) and HCUAE (UAE, services, VAT-registered). The next one can be a new country.

**Payments today (owner, 2026-10-25):** the website is down. Clients pay straight into the franchise's bank account, from a same-country or a foreign account. **Every payment is entered by an admin.** This feature builds the admin path only; the online parts are listed under "Deferred until online payments return".

**Local data vs production (verified 2026-10-25):** in the local DB all three franchises carry EFMUM's GSTIN `27CSEPS5397E1Z8`, and HCUAE has no TRN. Production is clean: neither Mahi nor HCUAE carries EFMUM's GSTIN there (owner). HCUAE's real TRN must still be entered before its VAT sales work (release runbook).

## User Stories

- As the **owner**, I want to say in Tax Master how each kind of sale is taxed (franchise × place-of-supply country × services or goods × how the money arrives × electronic or not × B2B or B2C), so that a CA or adviser decision becomes a configuration change, not code.
- As the **owner**, I want to record each franchise's tax registrations, including one in another country, with the currency each works in, so that the system knows what each entity may charge, print and book.
- As a **finance admin**, I want the system to flag hard when the evidence for a zero-rated export doesn't hold, so that I decide on the treatment and my decision is logged.
- As **Accounts**, I want customer GSTINs validated and B2B sales to use the GSTIN's State, and the shipping bill recorded on goods exports, so that returns and refunds are supported.
- As the **owner**, I want to sell an automated (electronic) plan into a country where we hold a registration, with that country's tax invoice in its currency, so that the product can launch when it exists.
- As a **partner franchise admin**, I want to see and maintain only my own franchise's tax setup, so that EatFit247's configuration stays private (principle 3).
- As a **super admin**, I want to switch each email / WhatsApp template on or off from the admin, so that, for example, the welcome email starts only when I activate it.

## Scope

**In scope**
- **Tax registrations master:** per franchise × country, with State, IEC, effective dates, **currency**, and the prefix, format and FY start of a foreign registration's series.
- **Franchise working currency:** `currency_code` on `mst_franchises`.
- **Treatment rules in Tax Master:** conditions plus a treatment (D3), seeded to reproduce today's tax outcomes.
- **Engine refactor:** a rule resolver plus treatment executors; mechanics and legal invariants stay in code (D6).
- **Save-time guardrails (D7)** and a **rule preview** in admin.
- **RBAC:** Tax Master and registrations become franchise-scoped; franchise admins edit their own franchise's rules; new `TaxEvidence` and `EmailTemplate` permissions (D14).
- **G7 evidence on admin entry; G15 customer GSTIN / TRN; G9 export documents.**
- **Foreign-registration supplies:** priced and invoiced in the registration's currency, in the registration's own series, with credit notes.
- **Invoice counters per franchise:** present for every active franchise; created only by migration or admin; a missing counter fails the entry; next-FY counters by admin action, with a reminder email (D12).
- **Default rules** for all plans or all products.
- **Retirement of the `mst_configs` switches** in D15 (`DIET_SAC_CODE` stays).
- **Welcome email:** a sender for `member_welcome`, shipped **inactive**, plus an admin Email Templates screen to switch templates on and off (D18).

**Out of scope**
- The Indian-client refusal for non-Indian franchises stays a **code invariant** (owner; mission.md markets table).
- Editing email template content in the admin (on/off switches only).
- Promo codes priced only in INR; IP or geo-IP evidence; GST bill-to-ship-to goods (s.10(1)(b)).
- The rest of the GSTR-1 workbook (4.13), VAT-201, UAE e-invoicing, GST credit and debit notes (4.9).
- **Online-payment work** (deferred list). Public checkout keeps working on the refactored engine with no new inputs.
- Changing invoices already issued (principle 10).
- **US sales tax: the next spec** (owner, 2026-10-25). The US has no VAT. Sales tax is set per State (none statewide in AK, DE, MT, NH, OR) and owed only where the seller has nexus (South Dakota v. Wayfair, 2018), and States differ on whether services or digital services are taxable. That spec needs: a specific-State rule condition; one sales-tax permit per State in the registrations table, with "no permit in that State = no tax"; the per-State rate moved from `mst_states.tax_percentage` (`us-sales-tax.service.ts`) into Tax Master rules; and a decision on city / county rates. Until then the SALES_TAX branch is unchanged. This spec's registrations table and franchise currency don't block it.
